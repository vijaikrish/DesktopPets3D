- "BirdWingFlap.wav" by nicStage - https://freesound.org/s/424463/ - CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) - cut into short wing-beat clips.

## Derived / synthesised

- `contact_call_*`, `alarm_*`: composed series of the single calls above (timing and +-4 % pitch varied).
- `growl_*`: beak rasp from 860519, slowed x0.6, low-passed, tremolo. No free sun-conure growl recording was found.
- `sneeze_*`, `sigh_*`: synthesised noise.

## Not used

- xeno-canto has 15 wild sun parakeet recordings, but the species is access-restricted (anti-trapping) and the files are CC BY-NC-SA.
- Green-cheeked conure recordings (867544, 856994, 856996) are a different genus (Pyrrhura): excluded.
- 693932 is melon seeds (CC0): only its 3 sharpest cracks are used, next to 7 sunflower cracks from 522138.
